A planner that learns

Daylit plans your day around your calendar, replans when the day changes, and learns from how your days really go.

Say what's on your mind. Daylit turns it into a realistic plan.

When the day changes, it proposes a new plan. Nothing changes until you apply it.

In the evening, tell it how the day went. Over time it learns which times work for you and how long things really take.

Private by design. Understanding and learning happen on your devices, and your data syncs only through your private iCloud.

For iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. Coming to the App Store.
