Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Daylit is built so your plans stay yours. We don’t run servers that store your data, we don’t sell or share it, and there are no ads or third-party analytics.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, goals, Inbox, lists, places, profile, and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync through your private iCloud account, which only you can read.

Calendar and location

Daylit reads your calendar to plan around your events and uses your location only to alert you near stores you saved. Neither leaves your device.

What Daylit learns

Daylit learns from what you say happened — which times work for you and how long things really take. It keeps a history of those updates and works things out on your devices; the history syncs only through your private iCloud. The words of a day review stay on this device, in History, for 7 days. Nothing new leaves your devices. See, change or reset everything in Settings → What Daylit knows, and turn learning off there any time.

AI

With Apple Intelligence, understanding what you capture, planning, and your weekly summary happen on your device. Only if you choose OpenAI in Settings and add your own key, the text you capture, and task titles with busy times when planning, are sent to OpenAI under their terms.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Daylit sees whether you’re subscribed, never your payment details.

Diagnostics

Daylit keeps simple usage counts (such as which days you opened it) and Apple’s crash and performance reports on your device, in Settings → Help → Diagnostics. Nothing is sent automatically; you can share them with support if you choose.

Your choices

Export everything — including your history and what Daylit learned from — or delete all data any time in Settings → Your data. Deleting removes it on every device. Questions: use Send feedback in Settings, or email kavya.sangisetty@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its date.
