Support

We're glad to help.

Email kavya.sangisetty@icloud.com. In the app, Settings → Help → Send feedback attaches the details that help us answer (app version and device); you see everything before it's sent.

Subscriptions

Daylit Premium is billed through your Apple Account. To cancel or change a plan, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, or the App Store on your Mac. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings → Subscription → Restore in Daylit.

Sync between devices

Sign in with the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud for Daylit on each device. Your plans sync through your private iCloud.

Your data

Export everything, or delete all your data, in Settings → Your data. See, change or reset what Daylit has learned in Settings → What Daylit knows.

Something isn't working

Update to the latest version of Daylit and of iOS, iPadOS, macOS or watchOS. If it still happens, send feedback from the app or email us, and tell us what you expected and what happened.
